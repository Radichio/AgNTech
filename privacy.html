Privacy policy

As at 27 July 2026

AgNtech Connect is a division of Tetra Farms Ltd., based in Manitoba, Canada. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, where it is held, and what you can ask us to do with it. We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect, and when

When you start a conversation

The website has several routes in — for those placing capital, raising or scaling a venture, holding agricultural exposure as a lender, or registering an interest in future roles. Each route asks a short set of questions before any details are taken. Depending on the route, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and organisation
  • Your answers to the screening questions for that route
  • Sector, stage, geography, timeline, and the nature of what you are seeking
  • A short description of your mandate, venture, or situation, and any note you add
  • For career registrations: your current role, a link you choose to share, and your availability

We ask for this because Terry reviews every enquiry personally and needs enough context to give a straight answer. You choose what to write; nothing beyond your name and email is required for us to reply.

When you book a time

Booking is handled by Calendly, and a meeting link is created through Google Meet. Your name, email address, and any note you add on booking are held by those services under their own privacy terms, and appear in our calendar.

When you use Ask Terry

Questions put to the site's AI assistant are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. Do not enter confidential deal information, personal financial details, or anything you would not put in a first email. The assistant offers general commentary and is not advice.

Automatically

Our host, Netlify, records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, as part of serving and securing the site. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Where your information is held

Enquiry details are stored in a Supabase database hosted in Canada (ca-central-1), and are also sent to Terry by email through Google Workspace. The website itself is served by Netlify. Some of these providers operate infrastructure outside Canada, and information held by them may be subject to the laws of those countries.

How we use it

  • To assess your enquiry and reply to you
  • To prepare for a conversation, if there is one
  • To keep a record of enquiries and the work that follows from them
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations

We do not sell your information. We do not pass it to third parties for their own marketing. Where an introduction to a third party is the point — for example, introducing an opportunity to a source of capital — we ask you first.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are kept for up to two years, so that we recognise you if you come back. Records connected to an engagement are kept for at least seven years after it ends, as professional and tax obligations require. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, and we will unless we are required to keep it.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct anything inaccurate, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent for us to hold it. Write to terry@agntechconnect.com and we will respond within thirty days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

Access to enquiry records requires an individual login, and the database is protected by row-level access controls. Email is protected by SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise — but we do not collect more than we need, and we do not keep it longer than we should.

Children

This site is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about information we hold, go to terry@agntechconnect.com, or write to AgNtech Connect, a division of Tetra Farms Ltd., Dauphin, Manitoba, Canada.

This policy describes how this website handles personal information. It is not legal advice, and it does not form part of any engagement between you and AgNtech Connect. If we work together, the terms of that work are set out in a separate written agreement.
