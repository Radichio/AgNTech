Careers & talent

Build the next thing in Canadian agriculture.

Manitoba is where agriculture and technology meet on working farmland — and where the people who build that future are needed most. If that is the work you want, AgNtech would like to know you.

There are no open positions today. Register your interest and yours is the first name looked at when a role opens — in the lab, or with the companies AgNtech works alongside.
Why here

The work is real, and the field is right outside.

Agriculture technology gets tested here in the conditions it was built for — real acres, real equipment, real seasons. You are close enough to the operation to see whether an idea holds up, and close enough to the people running it to hear why.

Manitoba’s agriculture and technology companies need people the wider market has not sent them. AgNtech knows those companies, and brings capital and people to them both.

Coming from away

If you are moving to Canada.

Skilled technologists, engineers, and operators are welcome here, wherever you are starting from. Manitoba offers room to build, a shorter path to the people who make decisions, and a cost of living that lets a career compound. Tell us where you are and what you do — the conversation starts there.

The development lab

Where a concept earns its keep in the field.

AgNtech is building a development lab — somewhere to test and prove new agriculture technology on working farmland rather than on paper. Trials run through real seasons on real acres, and results are measured by what holds up. As the lab grows, so does the team around it, and the first roles open there.

The shape of the team
